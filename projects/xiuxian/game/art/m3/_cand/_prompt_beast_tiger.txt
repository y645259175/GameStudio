Chinese ink wash painting (水墨写意), full-body character portrait of a 'Crimson-Maned Spirit Tiger' (赤鬃灵虎) — a fierce demonic spirit tiger from a cultivation game. The tiger is in a powerful crouching/snarling stance, body angled at 3/4 view, painted with bold sumi brush strokes and dry-brush feibai texture. Defining feature: thick CINNABAR RED flame-like mane on the neck/spine (this is the only red element, used boldly), the rest of the fur painted in ink-black wash with subtle stripes. Eyes glowing with ancient bronze warmth, mouth slightly open showing fangs but NOT bloody. Composition: full body centered, fills ~70% of the square frame, with a faint suggestion of mountain rocks or wisps of qi at the feet, mostly empty rice paper background. Fierce and powerful but with traditional ink-painting elegance, NOT gore, NOT horror. Style: traditional Chinese ink wash, brushy painterly, NO clean cartoon outlines, every form a brush stroke. Same brush language as the reference master/disciple portraits but rendered as a beast. Palette strictly limited to: rice-paper beige background, ink black (dominant fur), cinnabar red (mane only, bold), ancient bronze (eyes & subtle highlights), faint mountain blue-gray (only as atmospheric mist if any). NO anime, NO furry, NO 3D, NO photoreal, NO cute mascot, NO blood gore, NO text or logos.
