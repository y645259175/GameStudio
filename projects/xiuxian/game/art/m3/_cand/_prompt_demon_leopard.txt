Chinese ink wash painting (水墨写意), full-body character portrait of a 'Gold-Eyed Demon Leopard' (金瞳妖豹) — a sleek demonic leopard from a cultivation game. The leopard is in a stalking/prowling stance, body angled at 3/4 view, painted with bold sumi brush strokes and dry-brush feibai texture. Defining feature: piercing golden-bronze (古铜) glowing eyes (the focal point of the entire piece). Fur painted in ancient-bronze (warm brown-bronze) ink wash for the body, with subtle ink-black rosette spots in painterly brush dabs. Mouth closed, an aura of yaoqi (faint ink mist) curling around the body. Composition: full body centered in a low stalking pose, fills ~70% of the square frame, with faint qi-mist at the feet, mostly empty rice paper background. Sleek and predatory, an ancient demon-cat spirit. NOT cute, NOT a household pet, NOT a mascot. Style: traditional Chinese ink wash, brushy painterly, every form a brush stroke, NO clean cartoon outlines. Match the brush language of the reference character portraits but for a beast. Palette strictly limited to: rice-paper beige background, ancient bronze (dominant fur), ink black (rosette spots, shadow, qi mist), cinnabar red ONLY as a tiny accent (e.g., faint glow at fang tips or a tiny mark), the eyes themselves are GLOWING golden-bronze (this is the key visual). NO anime, NO 3D, NO photoreal, NO cute, NO blood gore.
